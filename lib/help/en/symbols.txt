Symbols On Your Map
===================

The symbols on your map can be divided into three categories: Dungeon
features such as walls, floors, doors and traps; Objects that can be
picked up such as treasure, weapons, magical devices, etc.; and creatures
that may or may not move around the dungeon, but which are mostly harmful
to your character's well-being.

Some symbols are used to represent more than one type of entity, and some
symbols are used to represent entities in more than one category. The symbol
"@" (by default) is used to represent the character.

You will not need to remember all the symbols and their meanings.
The "look" command ('/') will identify any character that appears on your
map (see 'commands.txt').

Note that you can use a "user preference file" to change
any of these symbols to something you feel more comfortable with.



Features that do not block line of sight
--------------------------------------------------

.  A floor space                 1  General Store entrance
.  A trap (hidden)               2  Armoury entrance
^  A trap (known)                3  Weaponsmith entrance
;  A glyph of warding            4  Bookseller entrance
'  An open door                  5  Alchemy shop entrance
'  A broken door                 6  Magic shop entrance
<  A staircase up                7  Black Market entrance
>  A staircase down              8  Your Home entrance
#  A lava pool


Features that block line of sight
-----------------------------------------------

#  A secret door                 #  A wall
+  A closed door                 %  A mineral vein
+  A locked door                 *  A mineral vein with treasure
:  A pile of rubble               :  A passable pile of rubble


Objects
-------

!  A potion (or flask)           /  A polearm
?  A scroll (or book)            |  An edged weapon
,  A mushroom (or food)          \  A hafted weapon
-  A wand or rod                 }  A sling, bow or crossbow
_  A staff                       {  A shot, arrow or bolt
=  A ring                        (  Soft armor
"  An amulet                     [  Hard armor
$  Gold or gems                  ]  Miscellaneous armor
~  Lights, Tools, Chests, etc    )  A shield
&  Multiple objects
