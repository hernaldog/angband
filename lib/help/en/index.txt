the symbols you may encounter in the game. For more help, see:

Please choose one of the following help files:

    (a) Available commands
    (b) Available symbols




Browser/help commands:
  #     go to line           %  go to file           ?  go to last file/menu
  SPACE advance 1 page       -  back 1 page           /  find text
  RET   advance 1 line       =  back 1 line           &  show (highlight) text
  +     advance 1/2 page     _  back 1/2 page         !  toggle case sensitivity
